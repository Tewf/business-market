# Market size, competition, platforms and personalisation: a first-pass review

A first-pass multivocal review, read on 2026-11-04. It asks how far a top-down and a bottom-up market size fall apart and what lasts as a difference. It asks when a venture is a two-sided market, and what tailoring a product does to demand, cost and rivalry. It ends on a named baseline.

- It holds 40 sources: 32 formal and 8 grey, two of the grey below the threshold and serving as examples of practice only, and one not scored.
- Grey: 4 tier 1, 1 tier 2 and 3 tier 3. No study of the error of a venture's market size was found.
- Formal: 13 measured, 9 supported with caveat, 2 convention and 8 convention argued from a model. Most numbers are read from abstracts and marked (secondary).

## Named baseline

- **B1, a market sized by two methods with intervals, and a competition frame of four questions.**
- A bottom-up size from segments, a top-down size from a published market and a share, every input an interval tagged PROVISIONAL; the gap reported; the premise behind it named ([baseline](baseline.md)).
- The program `checks/local/market_size.py` computes the sizes and the gap.
